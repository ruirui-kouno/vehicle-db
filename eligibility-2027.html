2027 US 25-Year Rule Eligibility Calendar

When the last 2002-built Japanese cars become importable to the United States — month by month. The clock runs from the month and year of manufacture on the car's Japanese Export Certificate, not the model year, so eligibility arrives in a rolling wave through 2027, not all at once.

The August 2027 wave

The final 2002 builds all cross the line by August 2027

Four Japanese sports cars ended production in August 2002 — the Nissan Skyline GT-R (R34), the Nissan Silvia (S15), the Mazda RX-7 (FD3S) and the Toyota Supra (JZA80; Toyota's own pages give July or August 2002). A car built in August 2002 turns 25 in August 2027. That makes August 2027 the last month of the 2002 wave: after it, every 2002-built car of these models is importable.

  • January – July 2027: 2002-built cars keep rolling in, month by month. A car built in March 2002 becomes eligible in March 2027 — the wave is gradual, not a single date.
  • August 2027: the final builds cross. The last R34 GT-Rs, S15 Silvias and RX-7s — including the 1,500-unit RX-7 Spirit R run — all become eligible.
  • September – December 2027: every 2002 car is eligible. The next wave is the 2003 builds, starting January 2028 (Lancer Evolution VIII and others).

Model-by-model: when each run fully clears

"All units eligible by" is the end-of-production month plus 25 years. Individual cars become eligible earlier — in the month they were built, 25 years on. Always verify a specific car by its manufacture month (build plate / Export Certificate), not by model year.
